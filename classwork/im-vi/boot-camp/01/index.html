Johannesburg, often called Jo'burg or Jozi, is the largest city in South Africa and one of the 50 largest urban areas in the world. It was founded in 1886 following the discovery of gold. The city is home to the tallest building in Africa, the Carlton Centre, and has more than 10 million trees, making it one of the world's largest man-made forests. Johannesburg is also known for its vibrant arts scene and is the economic powerhouse of Africa.
